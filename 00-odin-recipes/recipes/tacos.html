Description

Tacos are a traditional Mexican dish consisting of a folded or rolled tortilla filled with various ingredients such as meat, beans, cheese, and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Tortilla
  • Meat (beef, chicken, or fish)
  • Beans
  • Cheese
  • Vegetables (onions, tomatoes, lettuce)

Instructions

  1. Cook the meat in a skillet until fully cooked. Season with your choice of spices.
  2. Warm the tortillas in a pan or microwave.
  3. Assemble the tacos by placing the cooked meat on the tortilla, then adding beans, cheese, and vegetables as desired.
  4. Serve with salsa, sour cream, or guacamole if desired.
