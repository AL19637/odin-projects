Description

Lasagna is a traditional Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add ground beef and cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and season with pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese with a little pepper.
  6. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce. Layer with noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella cheese, and repeat until all ingredients are used, ending with a layer of meat sauce topped with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  8. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
