Description

Curry is a flavorful dish that originated in South Asia and is now enjoyed worldwide. It typically consists of a combination of spices, herbs, and other ingredients such as meat, vegetables, or legumes, cooked in a sauce.

Ingredients

  • Meat (chicken, beef, lamb, or tofu for a vegetarian option)
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Tomatoes
  • Coconut milk or yogurt
  • Spices (such as curry powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander, and chili powder)
  • Vegetables (such as potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and peas)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onions, garlic, and ginger, and sauté until onions are translucent.
  2. Add the meat (or tofu) and cook until browned on all sides.
  3. Stir in the spices and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until they break down and form a sauce.
  5. Pour in coconut milk or yogurt, and add vegetables. Simmer until the meat is cooked through and the vegetables are tender.
  6. Serve hot with rice or bread.
